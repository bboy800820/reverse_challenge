# Photographic cockpit assets

Generated 2026-10-28 with the built-in imagegen tool, using the user-supplied local file `assets/example` as a visual reference. Both calls requested `transparent_background: true`. These are generated derivatives, not a reconstruction or a claim that the source photograph is an exact car model. The original reference was not modified or bundled into the website. Images are used at their original generated resolution; alpha is preserved. Manifest records exact hashes.

## dashboard.png — final prompt

Create a production-ready photorealistic cockpit foreground PNG for an interactive driving simulator, using the attached car interior photograph as the visual reference. Landscape 1536x1024 composition. Match its convincing photographic black leather, integrated dashboard, silver trim, recessed analog instrument binnacle, inset rectangular infotainment screen, and left-hand-drive perspective. This is a layered game asset, not a whole screenshot. Keep only the interior dashboard in the LOWER 44 percent, subtle A pillars at extreme left and right, and narrow dark headliner across the top 4 percent. The entire windshield and side window openings must be genuinely transparent alpha, with no trees, scenery, glass tint, checkerboard graphic or opaque fill. REMOVE the rearview mirror entirely (live mirror will be added by code). REMOVE the steering wheel entirely (a separate rotating wheel will be added by code); reconstruct a natural dark lower dashboard/steering column behind it. Driver instrument cluster centered at x=30% y=68%, as a dark recessed oval housing with completely unlit blank black interior, ready for live instruments. Center infotainment display at x=73% y=73%, as a blank unlit black rectangular inset screen with silver bezel, ready for live video overlay. Dashboard upper edge at y=56% approximately, smooth continuous realistic form like reference, no floating tablets. Natural soft neutral daylight and detailed leather grain and stitching. Interior remains opaque all along bottom edge. No steering wheel, no rearview mirror, no road or outside scenery, no text, no UI, no watermarks. Preserve the photographic realism of the reference, avoid a simplified CGI dashboard.

## wheel.png — final prompt

Use the attached car interior photo as the design reference to create ONE isolated steering wheel asset for an interactive driving simulator. Photorealistic black stitched leather round wheel with thick padded rim, realistic grain, brushed silver three-spoke trim, physical multifunction buttons, black central airbag hub with an unbranded subtle inset shield (no letters or logo). Entire complete circular wheel visible, centered exactly in a square 1024x1024 canvas, outer rim occupies 90 percent of canvas width and height, circle center at pixel 512,512. Frontal face-on view suitable for smooth 2D rotation, almost no perspective distortion. Soft neutral daylight from above. Genuinely transparent alpha everywhere outside the wheel AND through all gaps between rim, spokes, and hub. No dashboard, column, stalks, instruments, hands, background, shadow on backdrop, text, numbers or watermark. Match the reference photograph's natural black leather interior realism.

## Runtime composition

`photo-cabin.ts` fits the original generated composition to the driver viewport, clips live mirror and monitor apertures, and adds separate dynamic SVG instruments plus the rotating wheel. The exterior remains Three.js. The previous modeled cockpit is the fallback if either image cannot load. Sizes/placement follow the delivered images, not the requested dimensions (the generated wheel is 1254×1254).
